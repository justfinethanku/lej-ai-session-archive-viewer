## Practical limits

Refresh rebuilds enabled stores, so a large archive takes minutes and source failures can leave partial results. It does not provide an atomic whole-archive replacement. Active writers can exhaust snapshot retries; refresh again during a quiet moment. Discovery creates private database snapshots even before indexing is enabled. Disabling a source removes its searchable records on refresh but retains snapshots/imported originals in app data. Formats are internal and can change. Cursor JSONL timestamps are unavailable. Attachment rendering, cross-app deduplication, cloud sync and secret redaction are outside this version.
